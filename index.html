Your pre-call reference for Marathon Petroleum discovery conversations

This tool helps you walk into any MPC conversation understanding how a refinery works, what questions to ask, and what your team has already learned. Four sections:

Process Explorer Tap any refinery unit — CDU, FCC, hydrocracker, DCS, and more — to see what it does, the KPIs that drive decisions there, and ready-to-use discovery questions for that unit.

Discovery Questions The full question bank organized by intent: Discovery (understand their landscape), Tech Fit (where your capabilities apply), and Pain Points (where deals start).

Business Segments MPC has three distinct P&Ls — Refining & Marketing, Midstream (MPLX), and Renewable Diesel. Each has different buyers and different tech priorities. Know which one you're talking to.
📋
Field Intel Real notes from client conversations — what we've heard, who said it, and what it means for our approach. Admins keep this updated. Read it before every call.
